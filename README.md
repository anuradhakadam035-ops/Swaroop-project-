## 🌟 About The Project

Choosing the right career can be difficult when students have limited information about their skills, interests, and available career opportunities.

The **AI Career Guidance System** solves this problem by using an intelligent assessment system to analyze user responses and recommend suitable career paths.

> 💡 **"Don't choose a career randomly. Choose a career based on your potential."**

The platform evaluates users through carefully designed **MCQ-based assessments** and generates personalized career recommendations.
